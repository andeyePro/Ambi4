# andeye Contributor Licence Agreement

**Version 1.0 — 26 July 2026.** This is the contributor licence agreement
for all andeye Ltd repositories. The copy of this agreement inside each
repository (its `CLA.md`, which carries this version number) is the text you
agree to when you contribute to that repository.

> **In plain terms** (this box is a friendly summary, not the agreement –
> the numbered clauses below are what actually binds):
>
> - You keep your copyright. Nothing here signs your work away.
> - Your contribution stays available under the open-source licence of the
>   repository you contributed it to. That release can't be taken back.
> - You additionally let andeye Ltd relicense your contribution, including
>   under proprietary terms. This is what lets the same code ship both as
>   open source and in builds the open-source licence couldn't reach – for
>   example the App Store, which cannot distribute AGPL software – and lets
>   andeye maintain licence exceptions (such as clarifying that a
>   program's *output* belongs to its users).
> - You grant a patent licence covering your contribution, and confirm the
>   work is yours to give.
> - Signing is one line in your first pull request, plus your name in
>   the repository's CONTRIBUTORS.md. No forms, no scanning, no wet
>   signature.
> - This agreement is governed by Scots law; any dispute goes to
>   arbitration in Glasgow.
>
> This document was drafted with AI assistance and is not legal advice.
> Read the clauses before relying on it; if you are contributing on behalf
> of an employer or your own company, check clauses 5 and 6 apply to you.

An agreement in the style of the Apache ICLA / Harmony agreements. It
exists because andeye Ltd dual-licenses its codebases (an open-source
licence in the public repository, other terms where that licence cannot
reach), which requires the company to hold sufficient rights in every
contribution.
